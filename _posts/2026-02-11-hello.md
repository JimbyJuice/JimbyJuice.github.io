## Verification
### Static Verification
Testing before executing the code, also known as compile-time checks.
Examples include: Type checking. 
### Dynamic Verification
Testing whilst executing the code, also known as run-time checks.
- Can be smart and large scale.

#### Blackbox Testing
- Testing without knowing the processes/functions.
- In other words, we can write tests for functions without the knowing the code, as we know the inputs and outputs.
